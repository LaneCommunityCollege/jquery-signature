Add your signature to Gmail

  1. On your computer, enter your information above and review the signature preview.
  2. Click Copy to Clipboard.
  3. Open your Lane account in Gmail. Click the Settings gear, then See all settings.
  4. On the General tab, scroll to Signature. Select the signature you want to update, or click Create new and give it a name, such as Lane.
  5. Click inside the signature editing box and replace its existing contents by pasting your generated signature. Use Ctrl+V on Windows or Command+V on a Mac to preserve the formatting.
  6. Under Signature defaults, select your signature for new emails and, if desired, replies and forwards.
  7. Scroll to the bottom of the settings page and click Save Changes.
  8. Start a new message to check the signature, then send yourself a test email to review its appearance.

If the copy button does not work, select the signature in the preview, copy it with Ctrl+C or Command+C, and paste it into Gmail.

Google's Gmail signature instructions

Using Gmail on your phone?

Check your mobile signature settings. If you have not set a separate signature in the Gmail app, new messages use the signature you set up on your computer. A separate mobile signature applies to messages sent from the app and needs to be updated separately.

To check the settings for your Lane account:

  • Android: Open the Gmail app, tap Menu, then Settings, choose your Lane account, and tap Mobile signature.
  • iPhone or iPad: Open the Gmail app, tap Menu, then Settings. Under Compose and Reply, tap Signature settings. Check the setting for your Lane account if you have multiple accounts.

After saving your computer signature, send yourself a new message and a reply from your phone to confirm the signature you want is included.

See Google's instructions for Android signatures or iPhone and iPad signatures.
